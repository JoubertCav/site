Working paper

Testing Spatial Stationarity under Unobserved Institutional Shocks: A Nonparametric Counterfactual Approach

Institutional shocks are seldom recorded, which is why spatial stationarity is difficult to test. Rather than assume a functional form for shocks nobody observes, the test is built from counterfactual comparisons. Currently under review.
